# DesiSquare: new-member welcome sequence

**Trigger:** a new member joins.
**Goal:** read → ask a first question or give a first answer → visit weekly.
**Exit:** when they post a first question or answer, skip ahead to email 4.
**Every email ends with:** `Educational community · Not investment advice.`
**Rules:**
- Say "Guru", never "maven".
- Never rank people by money.
- Never mention or ask for a phone number. WhatsApp is only an opt-in in Settings.
- Say "100+ tools".
- Never use the member's real name if they joined pseudonymously; greet them by display name: `{{display_name}}`.

| # | Day | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Welcome + how standing works |
| 2 | 2 | Ask your first question |
| 3 | 5 | Free tools: NRO alarm, FBAR check |
| 4 | 9 | Answer someone (standing comes from helping) |
| 5 | 14 | Your privacy, in plain words |

### 1 · Day 0: Welcome
**Subject:** Welcome to the Square · *A/B:* Money questions. Desi answers.
**Preview:** Here, helpfulness earns standing, never money.
