# DesiSquare: post text for every asset

**Product rules for every post**
- End with `Educational community · Not investment advice.`
- Say **Guru**, never "maven".
- Never rank or brag by money: no returns in captions, no "top performers".
- Pseudonymous, percent-only, private flags, WhatsApp only with consent.
- Say "100+ tools" (the app shows 102 in one place and 103 in another).
- The questions shown come from the demo community; call them examples, not testimonials.
