# Swaad: post text for every asset

**Rules for every post**
- Never "organic", never "Made in USA". Everything is made in the Chennai kitchen and shipped across the US.
- No prices (they're placeholders). No health claims. No delivery-speed or "free shipping" promises.
- Festival dates come from the store's calendar. Before boosting, check each product's order-by date on the product page.
- Product descriptions are the catalog's own blurbs.
- Instagram leads; LinkedIn tells the BigMo operating story.
- Link: ecommerce-store.big-mo.ai ("link in bio" on Instagram).
