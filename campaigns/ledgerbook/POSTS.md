# Ledger Book: post text for every asset

**Rules**
- The AI never posts: it proposes, a person approves, and the ledger enforces balance.
- The tax set-aside is a rule of thumb, never tax advice.
- No competitor named, no "replaces your accountant", no time-saved or accuracy figures.
- Every amount shown is demo data.

Six rules for letting an AI near your books, and keeping it out of the journal:
1. The model proposes. It never posts.
2. Show the reason, not just the answer.
3. Debits equal credits, enforced not hoped.
4. Posted means posted.
5. Store nothing you can compute.
6. Give the copilot read-only tools.

Why our AI bookkeeper can't post to your books:

A model that's 95% right is wonderful for sorting transactions and terrible as the final say on a ledger. So in Ledger Book the model only proposes. The rules that make books trustworthy (balanced entries, immutability, reversals) are enforced by code, and a person approves anything the model isn't sure about.
