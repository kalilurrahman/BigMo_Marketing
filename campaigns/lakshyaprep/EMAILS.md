# LakshyaPrep: "Lock the goal" email sequence

**Trigger:** a free account is created or the free scan is started.
**Goal:** scan finished → goal score and date locked → book 1:1 coaching (Lock my goal).
**Exit:** as soon as the student books coaching or buys a plan, leave the sequence and move to onboarding. If the scan is finished early, skip email 2.
**Voice:** hype coach with receipts. Subject lines ≤ 6 words. Emoji only 🎯 🔥 ✅. Never guarantee a score or admission, and never quote a result.
**Branch:** if the sign-up came from a parent page or the parent role, send the **parent track** at the end instead.

| # | Day | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 (instant) | Welcome + start the scan |
| 2 | 1 | Nudge to finish the scan (skip if done) |
| 3 | 3 | Lock a goal score + date |
| 4 | 6 | GoalGap: points, not percent |
| 5 | 9 | Free full-length practice test |
| 6 | 14 | Talk to a coach |

---

**Subject:** Study what pays · *A/B:* Not all mistakes cost the same
**Preview:** Why GoalGap ranks skills by points, not percent wrong.
```

Most prep tells you what you got wrong most often. GoalGap asks a better question: which skills can still give you the most points?

A skill you miss a lot but that's worth little can wait. A skill worth many points goes first. So every hour goes where your score can still move.
```
